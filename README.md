## Why BandwagonHost plans go out of stock

Two separate mechanisms are at play, and mixing them up is why people wait for plans that were never coming back that week.

BandwagonHost is run by IT7 Networks, a Canadian company that has been in the hosting business since 2004. It provisions VPS on hardware it owns — RAID-10 SSD arrays, with NVMe on the newer Hong Kong, DC9 and Vancouver nodes — and it does not oversell. When a datacenter's allocated slots for a tier fill up, usually the 1GB CN2 GIA-E tier, that tier shows out of stock until someone cancels or new hardware comes online. CN2 GIA bandwidth in particular is allocated in fixed pools per datacenter and simply can't be stretched, which is why the cheapest tiers in the most popular locations (DC6 CN2 GIA-E, DC9, Hong Kong) go red regularly even though recent hardware additions in Hong Kong, Los Angeles DC9 and Vancouver have reopened slots elsewhere.

Limited editions work on a completely different rhythm. THE PLAN, MINICHICKEN, THE CHICKEN, the Box family (MiniBox, BiggerBox, PowerBox, SakuraBox, BiggerBox Pro, MegaBox Pro), FREEDOM PLAN, The DC9 Plan, The Tokyo Plan — none of these are permanent catalog items. A batch drops, sells out (the $19/year MINICHICKEN went in a single morning), and then it's silence until the next restock. There is no published schedule. The $49.99/year CN2 GIA-E 10G limited edition has vanished for months at a stretch and then returned without any announcement.
